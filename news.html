  1. September 2026

    Research

    Spatial Proximity as a Discriminatory Factor for Acne-Identifying CNNs

    At Skinapse, we tested whether where acne lesions sit relative to one another can help a model classify them. Using the Clark–Evans test on the ACNE04 dataset, we found that spatial clustering and lesion-to-lesion proximity can raise the confidence of CNN models.

    CRISP Intern at Mayo Clinic

    I wrapped up my CRISP internship at Mayo Clinic by presenting a multi-site retrospective cohort analysis of survival, graft failure, and social determinants of health after solid organ transplantation.

    ADI, Skinapse & Intelli Schools

    My nonprofit, ADI, and my venture, Skinapse, partnered with Intelli Schools in Visakhapatnam, India, to teach adolescent students about their skin health. We talked through the challenges specific to darker skin tones, and I got to see firsthand how our image detection models performed on a new demographic.
